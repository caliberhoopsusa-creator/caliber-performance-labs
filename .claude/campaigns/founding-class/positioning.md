# Founding Class — Campaign Positioning

**Campaign:** Founding Class (waitlist drive, ~30 days)
**Goal:** Waitlist signups — first players + founding teams.
**Lead audience:** HS/AAU basketball players, recruited **as teams** through their coaches.

---

## The angle
**"The future of sports is measured."**
Every athlete trains on gut feel. Caliber turns every game into a position-weighted **A–F grade** and a **0–99 Caliber Score** — so you finally know exactly where you stand, what to fix, and recruiters can actually see it.

## One-liner
> Every game, graded. Know your caliber.

## Message hierarchy (say them in this order)
1. **Every game graded** — the core utility. Stop guessing.
2. **The Caliber Score (0–99)** — the flex. A number you earn and want to share.
3. **Get seen** — graded profiles surface to recruiters. The aspiration.
4. **Bring your team** — the cold-start mechanic. Founding programs get in first.

## Audience truth
- **Players (13–18):** want to get better, get ranked, get recruited. Motivated by the score + visibility.
- **Coaches:** the wedge — one coach onboards a whole roster. Pitch verification, team view, development.
- **Guardians/recruiters:** secondary now; ride along.

## Tone
Future-of-sports · confident · premium-athletic · honest.
Visual: obsidian black · silver · crimson; condensed athletic type (Big Shoulders / Barlow Condensed).
**Avoid:** fake user counts, fabricated testimonials, "trusted by 10,000+", corporate jargon, hype with no substance.

## Proof strategy (HONEST — early stage)
Lead with **product truth + scarcity**, never fake social proof:
- Product-truth metrics: **6** graded categories · **50+** skill badges · **A–F** every game · **<2 min** to log.
- **Founding-class scarcity:** "Be one of the first. Founding programs only."
- The **live engine demo** (the grade computing itself) as proof the thing works.

## CTA system
- **Primary (player):** "Join the founding class" → waitlist (email).
- **Team (coach):** "Bring your team — apply as a founding program."
- One CTA per asset. Earned, specific, never generic "Learn more."

## Taglines (rotate)
- Know your caliber.
- Every game, graded.
- The future of sports is measured.
- Train on data, not gut.
