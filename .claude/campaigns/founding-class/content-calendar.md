# Founding Class — 30-Day Action Plan (Jun 22 – Jul 22, 2026)

Goal: **waitlist signups** — first players + founding teams. Lead: players via teams.
Cadence is built for a solo/small founder at ~$0 (one small ad test in Week 3).

## Weekly themes
- **Week 1 (Jun 22–28) — Launch & seed.** "Every game, graded." Stand the waitlist up, announce, seed communities.
- **Week 2 (Jun 29–Jul 5) — Land founding teams.** Push the coach/team mechanic; onboard your first program.
- **Week 3 (Jul 6–12) — Amplify.** The score as the flex; turn the best Reel into a small ad test.
- **Week 4 (Jul 13–19) — Close the class.** Scarcity push; convert the waitlist.
- **Jul 20–22 — Review** and decide next phase.

## Recurring weekly rhythm (the engine)
| When | Action | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon / Wed / Fri · 9:00am** | Post short-form | Publish 1 Reel/TikTok from `social-posts.md`. Rotate: grade-reveal → utility → aspiration/team. |
| **Tue / Thu · 9:00am** | Coach outreach | DM 5 AAU/HS coaches — offer "founding program" (you log their first games). ~10/week. |
| **Tue / Thu · 4:00pm** | Founder posts + community | 2 X posts from `ad-copy.md` + genuinely help in 2 hoops communities (Reddit/FB/Discord). |
| **Fri · 4:00pm** | Weekly review & plan | Log waitlist signups, note best-performing post, plan next week's 3 Reels + 10 DMs. |

## Milestones (one-offs)
- **Mon Jun 22 · 9:00am — LAUNCH.** Publish the waitlist landing (`landing-page.md` copy), post the founder announcement, share to your networks.
- **Mon Jul 6 · 10:00am — Ad test.** Boost the top-performing Reel as Ad A + a retargeting ad (small budget). 
- **Mon Jul 20 · 10:00am — 30-day review.** Tally signups + founding teams, decide: open more spots / iterate / scale the winners.

## Success markers (honest, early-stage)
- Week 1: waitlist live + 25 signups + 10 coach DMs sent.
- Week 2: 1 founding team committed.
- Week 3: ad test running; best Reel > others by 3×.
- Week 4 / Day 30: 100+ waitlist, 1–3 active founding teams logging real games.

## Asset map
